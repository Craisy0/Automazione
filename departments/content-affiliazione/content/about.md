SoloStack reviews AI and automation tools specifically for freelancers, consultants and one-person businesses — not enterprise teams with a dedicated ops department.

Every recommendation is based on what actually saves time for someone running a business alone: less setup complexity, clear pricing, and tools that pay for themselves quickly. Some links on this site are affiliate links, which help keep the site independent and ad-light; they never change which tools we recommend.
